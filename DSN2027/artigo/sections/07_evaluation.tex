\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:eval}

We answer four research questions.
\textbf{RQ1:} Does a low-cost defender detect leaks hidden by frontier attackers?
\textbf{RQ2:} How much does the mechanical evidence of L2 help, i.e., defender with versus without
the L2 report?
\textbf{RQ3:} What does each layer contribute (ablation) and at what cost per verdict?
\textbf{RQ4:} What does confidential OCI execution cost compared with the previous executor, and
does it stop the attack suite?

\subsection{Setup}
\textbf{Battle.} Attackers are frontier models from \todo{lista exata de modelos} and large
open-weight models; defenders are low-cost open-weight models on cloud GPUs and low-cost API
models, plus at most one expensive defender. Every attacker meets every defender; the defender
does not know whether it receives a benign or a leaking version. The total API budget is
US\$100; we use prompt caching and cut repetitions before cutting matrix cells. Temperature is
fixed and each pair is repeated \tbd\ times to report variance.

\textbf{Platform.} Azure confidential VMs \todo{família SEV-SNP/TDX e tamanho}, single-use, from a
warm pool.

\textbf{Metrics.} Detection rate on valid attacks, false-positive rate on benign versions, cost per
verdict (tokens, time, US\$), attacker refusal rate, and for the platform the job latency by phase.
