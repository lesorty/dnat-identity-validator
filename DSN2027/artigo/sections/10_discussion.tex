\section{Discussion and Limitations}\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{What the gate does not see.} A backdoor that activates only on a trigger absent from the
battery, a new cipher the Judge does not decode, and multi-turn erosion beyond a few sentinel dialogs
are outside the property we test; the encoded items cover known encodings
only~\cite{halawi2024covert}. Weight export, distillation through many queries and side channels are
excluded because the weights never leave the platform and serving is locked to the judged template,
decoding and formats. The quota prices attempts but does not stop a wealthy attacker; a memory-hard
proof of work would narrow the hardware advantage.

\textbf{Toward external scrutiny.} Our adaptive attacker is an internal bounty with a declared budget;
an open bounty, scored by the secret battery, is the natural extension. The receipt binds each verdict
to the Judge's code and thresholds by hash, a step toward proof-of-guardrail claims that a third party
can check without trusting the operator, which remains the trusted party here.
