\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Confidential computing removes the operator from the trust base of data marketplaces but leaves
the application in it. We closed every channel except the legitimate output, with attested builds
and single-use CVMs that only talk to the platform, and turned the remaining question into a task
for an AI Judge that runs as an ordinary application of the platform. Its layered design gives a
low-cost LLM mechanical evidence that prompt injection cannot alter.
